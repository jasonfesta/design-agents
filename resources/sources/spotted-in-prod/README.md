## How the organization works

The homepage combines a shuffled app-icon grid, groups of tag links, and a large clip preview. Tag links open clip grids. App views collect clips for a product; details connect a clip to its app, team context, product category, and multiple tag groups. [Focus Flight](https://www.spottedinprod.com/apps/focusflight?activeClip=563), for example, has a settings clip tagged with Live Session, Settings, Context Menu, Blur, and Skeuomorphism. The source app metadata links to its [App Store entry](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6648771147).

The useful model is multiple independent classifications attached to the same reference. It lets us find a style, a visual material, a user action, or a pattern without copying the reference into every folder. Our [shared style registry](../../styles/README.md) now uses the 20 Design Language tags as the primary menu for both apps and websites. The other groups remain selectable subcategories or context within existing records.

## Scope and source limits

All seven expanded tag groups were inspected. The initial taxonomy study checked selected UI views and one app detail. The subsequent onboarding study inspected sampled frames from 39 clips; the full clip library was not reviewed or downloaded. Public homepage metadata also yielded 100 app records and [representative design-language references](references.md); these remain discovery evidence in the snapshot, not a claim of full app coverage.

Spotted in Prod focuses on iOS and contains both editorial app clips and community submissions. Presence on the site alone does not independently verify a release, accessibility, usability, implementation method, or current App Store availability. Website application of these tags is Jason’s requested vocabulary extension. Personal branch taste remains undefined.

The complete tag capture was available publicly. A free account was established in Chrome during the requested access check. Paid membership is not needed to capture this taxonomy. The later onboarding study used source media exposed by the playable gallery. No account identity or credentials are stored in this portable collection.

[Source history and refresh guidance](../../history/sources/spotted-in-prod/README.md) preserve attribution and response hashes. The saved onboarding media are local research references; ownership remains with their creators.
