## Reference direction

Jason endorsed the set containing Reelfolio, Inspora, 60fps, Mobbin, Land-book, Refero, Brand Guidelines, and Backgrounds Supply. ## Material notes

- **affectUI:** Official site inspected in the browser after the web reader could not fetch it.
- **beUI:** Public library states MIT; Pro uses a separate paid license.
- **EvilCharts:** Repository lists an MIT license.
- **Inspora:** Nero links Inspora in his profile and posts about adding designs and its launch. The user explicitly endorsed this resource set.
- **Kobra:** Homepage redirected to /components/input-otp, where source and a prompt.md tab were visible.
- **Mobbin:** Pricing snapshot on 2026-10-12: Pro $10/month billed yearly; Team $16/member/month billed yearly. Recheck before purchase.
- **Reelfolio:** It is a showreel tool; the original roundup description of generic portfolio hosting was imprecise.
- **Refero Styles:** Beta. Treat these as reference breakdowns rather than automatically assuming they are official design systems.
- **Transitions.dev:** Motion-agent scoring claims were read from the product page, not tested.
- **Designers and Machines:** The page listed October 29 as the next event when reviewed on 2026-10-12; event availability was not booked or verified.
- **F*cking Good Libraries:** User short link https://t.co/R5eFMrytkp resolved here.
- **Liquid Glass:** MIT was identified in the repository review. Live backdrop refraction in its wrapper is Chromium-specific; Safari/Firefox require duplicated refracted content. No runtime or browser-matrix test was performed.
- **Fluid Functionalism:** Use the upstream mickadesign repository, not the initially encountered fork.
- **shadercn:** Discovered through fglibs; requires WebGPU.
- **Recent:** The site describes roots in a collection of designs posted on X and continuity with Godly. Preserve historical domains until redirects are verified.
- **DesignEng:** The sharing account is a discovery source, not established as the creator. Do not merge with designengineer.tools without verifying identity.
- **Aave glass implementation article:** Article credits Lochie Axon; engineering Abhijeet Singh; design Alex Vanderzon and Ana Howard.
- **liquid-dom:** Experimental DOM rendering needs a Chrome feature flag; preserve as a research reference, not a production compatibility claim.
- **Planes:** Examples: /components/physical-knob and /components/receipt-printer. Free and Pro components. Homepage advertised $99 once and $199 after October 15, observed 2026-10-12; recheck before purchase. One-developer license stated.

## Evidence and open questions

- “OnDesign” remains unresolved. The crawl found open-design.ai and opendesign.dev, and DxA lists nexu-io/open-design; do not assume any is what Jason originally meant.
- All seven newly supplied sites were inspected: affectUI, Kobra, Arc UI, EvilCharts, Transitions.dev, beUI, and BoardUI. Most of the larger Nero capture remains post-verified discovery material.
- Library, skill, and MCP inclusion does not mean installed, configured, accessible on a paid tier, or tested. Preserve original license and upstream author attribution before reuse.
- Prices and counts are dated observations. Check product pages before making a spending or implementation decision.
- Earlier exploratory leads such as HeyDesigner, Kargul Studio’s swipe file, motion-primitives, and designeer.xyz were not promoted to the reviewed shortlist.
- Chrome history did not provide usable results. The catalogue comes from shared links, primary sites, repository references, and X research.

## Discovery workflow for the design agent

Read a creator’s relevant posts, expand resource lists, capture link-bearing self replies, and keep the original post URL. Follow useful sites to official docs and repositories. Deduplicate by canonical resource while retaining distinct evidence posts. Record source review, setup checks, and workflow testing separately. Classify visual direction, implementation fit, licensing, browser requirements, and the design decision the resource can help with. Existing team-watch records continue to use their own organization and event schema.
