Updated October 13, 2026 · Category describes the product; subcategory describes its interface.

Use `type/device/widthxheight/category/subcategory/style`. An app can contain several families, each linked to one product context. Finance → Dashboards and Finance → Checkout and payments describe different tasks in the same domain. SaaS marketing → Pricing and Puzzle → Level selection apply the same rule to other design types.

Keep the precise screen label, flow step, components, states, audience, business model, and source tags in the record. A transaction history can use `data-management`; a failure message is an error state, not a new product category. A component or section study stays attached to its screen family instead of creating another mandatory layer.

## Source vocabulary

Use [Spotted in Prod Flows](../sources/spotted-in-prod/taxonomy.md#flows) and [Patterns](../sources/spotted-in-prod/taxonomy.md#patterns) as source tags. Map inspected task families to the appropriate subcategory. Preserve the source’s exact labels and IDs; source product categories, UI patterns, and states are different axes. Source tag groups remain complete in the capture.

The website/game menus are proposed consolidations of previously recorded page/screen details. Resolve uncertain names through Atlas; do not infer personal taste from their presence. Earlier decisions remain in [History](../history/README.md).
