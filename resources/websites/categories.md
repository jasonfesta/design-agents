Jason specifically singled out **portfolios** for further category research on October 12, 2026. Start there, using the selected popular templates to compare narrower uses. The following research shortlist sits within the approved menu; it does not change the category IDs or rank market demand.

For portfolios, preserve the providers' actual labels too: Framer uses **Portfolio → Artists / Designers / Photography / Resume**; Wix uses **Portfolio & CV → Portfolios / Resumes & CVs / Personal**; Squarespace combines **Portfolio** with topic filters such as **Art & Design** and **Photography**. These source paths and the narrower uses above are record fields, not additional folder layers. A résumé-led personal site can be compared with project-led portfolios without assuming they need the same content structure.

The [popular-template research](../guides/template-market-research.md) owns the source links, observed collection order, dates, authors, AI capabilities, and inspection limits. Category interest does not select a visual style.

## Resolve overlap by the deliverable

These are practical browsing buckets; a SaaS site can contain a landing page. For a whole SaaS marketing site, use `saas-marketing` and record the exact page. For a standalone campaign or conversion-page study, use `landing-pages` with SaaS as product context. Choose one primary home for the actual record and cross-link related categories; do not duplicate an entire site for every applicable label.

An authenticated dashboard or editor belongs under **apps**, including when it runs in a browser. A SaaS product may therefore have related website and app records. Record their relationship. Login alone is not the deciding criterion: classify by the task and deliverable.

Pricing, Features, About, and Changelog now map to interface subcategories beneath the site category. FAQ may be a page or a section; record that scope and resolve a missing family through Atlas. This October 13 category/subcategory decision supersedes the earlier page-detail-only boundary.
