# Supporting research

[Home](../README.md) · [Workflows](../workflows/README.md)

Reading that helps designers make better decisions about agents. These are research and practice references, not installable tools. Source pages reviewed September 24, 2026.

| Reference | Kind | Why read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/) | Amershi et al., CHI 2019 research paper | A basis for reviewing how a product communicates capabilities, handles mistakes, and supports correction. |
| [Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) | Anthropic engineering article, originally 2024 | Distinguishes predefined workflows from agent-directed work and discusses the cost of complexity. Treat implementation details as historical unless rechecked. |

Our application of these references: prototype progress, uncertainty, correction, and recovery alongside the primary task. Record which recommendation comes from a source and which is the team's own interpretation.

Use these alongside [Design an agent interaction](../workflows/README.md#design-an-agent-interaction) and [Review before shipping](../workflows/README.md#review-before-shipping).
