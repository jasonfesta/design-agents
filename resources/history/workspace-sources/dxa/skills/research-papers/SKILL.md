# Research into practice

Start with the product decision or user question. Find primary research or first-party practice reports relevant to it, and read the source before summarizing.

Useful starting sources include [Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/) and [Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents). The second is an engineering article, not a controlled research study.

Separate the authors' findings, study conditions, and limitations from your interpretation. Record publication date and source. Do not promote an abstract, anecdote, or benchmark into a general claim about product quality.

Connect each useful finding to a concrete design decision and a way to check it. For example, turn a recommendation about correction into a prototype recovery state and a task-based review.

Return the sources, relevant findings, proposed application, and unresolved evidence. Avoid collecting unrelated papers merely to increase coverage.
