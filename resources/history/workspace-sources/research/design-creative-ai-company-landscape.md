# Design and Creative AI: Company and Model Landscape

_Research snapshot: August 19, 2026_

> “Design” includes product/UI design, brand and marketing production, image/video generation, vector graphics, 3D and creative workflow. Public leadership routes only; no personal emails are inferred.

## Executive overview

Design AI is converging from two directions. Incumbent workspaces—**Adobe, Canva and Figma**—are adding generation and agents to systems designers already use. Model-native companies—**Recraft, Krea, Runway, Ideogram, Black Forest Labs and Luma**—are building proprietary visual models and then wrapping them in creative workflow.

The Harvey/Tenet analogue is not merely an image generator. It is a system that internalizes a company's brand, taste, components, prior work and approval rules; generates editable assets; evaluates them against constraints; and learns from selection and revision behavior. **Recraft** and **Krea** are the clearest independent owned-model/workspace combinations. Adobe has the strongest licensed-data and enterprise-distribution position.

## The design RL environment

A useful design environment is not rewarded by generic “prettiness.” It must test brand compliance, hierarchy, readability, accessibility, component correctness, editability, conversion goals, novelty, manufacturability or production constraints. The strongest signals come from what expert designers select, reject and revise—and whether the final asset performs in the real channel.

Potential environments:

- Apply a brand system across a campaign while preserving editable layers.
- Generate UI screens using an existing component library and accessibility constraints.
- Iterate a concept from mood board to approved asset with reviewer feedback.
- Convert between image, vector, layout, video and 3D without losing structure.
- Optimize creative variants against downstream engagement or conversion.
- Produce physical designs that pass CAD, simulation and manufacturing checks.

## Diligence questions

- Are model weights owned, licensed or routed to third parties?
- What rights cover training data and customer customization?
- Are outputs editable objects or flattened pixels/video?
- How is brand/taste memory represented and isolated by tenant?
- What feedback signals alter future generations?
- Can outputs be traced, indemnified and cleared for enterprise use?
- Does evaluation measure professional task success or only aesthetic preference?
