# Medicine and Healthcare AI: Company and Model Landscape

_Research snapshot: August 19, 2026_

> Render in any Markdown viewer. Leadership and contact details are public-company information; no personal emails are inferred.

## Executive overview

Healthcare AI divides into clinical agents, ambient documentation, medical knowledge, diagnostics, care delivery, imaging, drug discovery and infrastructure. Unlike legal AI, success is constrained not only by accuracy and workflow adoption but by clinical safety, regulation, EHR integration, liability, privacy and prospective validation.

The closest healthcare analogues to Harvey/Tenet are **Hippocratic AI**, which owns the Polaris healthcare-model constellation; **Abridge**, which owns a medical speech and clinical-conversation stack and is developing a domain foundation model; and **Nabla**, which owns medically adapted speech/language models. **OpenEvidence** is the strongest physician research product, although public evidence about complete ownership of its core model is less explicit.

## Model-ownership view

| Strategy | Companies |
|---|---|
| Named proprietary healthcare foundation/constellation | Hippocratic AI/Polaris; Abridge clinical-conversation model in development |
| Proprietary clinical task-model stack | Abridge, Nabla, Ada, Ambience, DeepScribe, imaging vendors |
| Clinical corpus and retrieval with mixed model stack | OpenEvidence, Pathway, Glass Health, incumbent publishers |
| Foundation models for biology or molecules | Owkin, Generate, Profluent, Isomorphic Labs, Insilico Medicine |
| Workflow/data moat using external frontier models | Many ambient, operational and patient-engagement products |

## Training and RL environments

High-value environments include simulated patient conversations, nurse outreach, longitudinal care-plan execution, chart review, coding, evidence retrieval, diagnostic differentials and molecular design with wet-lab validation. Rewards must incorporate factuality, omission risk, escalation behavior, bedside manner, policy compliance and clinical outcomes—not only text similarity.

## Diligence questions

- Which model weights are owned, fine-tuned or merely routed?
- Can the vendor provide prospective clinical validation rather than retrospective benchmarks?
- Who reviews failures and how do corrections reach prompts, retrieval, policies or weights?
- Is the product decision support, autonomous action or regulated medical software?
- Are customer data, de-identified data and derivative signals used for training?
- Which EHR actions can the agent execute, and where is human approval mandatory?
