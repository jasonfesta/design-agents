# Cybersecurity AI: Company, Agent and Environment Landscape

_Research snapshot: August 19, 2026_

> Public leadership and company outreach only. Titles marked for verification may change quickly.

## Executive overview

Cybersecurity's strongest Harvey/Tenet analogues are not necessarily companies with a branded security LLM. The deeper moats are **executable environments**, **attack graphs**, **security telemetry**, **verified exploits**, **incident outcomes**, and policies learned from expert analysts and hackers.

The market has two agentic poles: offensive systems such as **XBOW** and **RunSybil**, which discover and validate attack paths, and defensive systems such as **Dropzone AI** and **Prophet Security**, which investigate alerts, hunt threats and engineer detections. Platform incumbents—Microsoft, Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike, Google Cloud/Mandiant and SentinelOne—can distribute similar agents through the installed security stack.

## RL-environment thesis

Cybersecurity is unusually well suited to reinforcement learning because actions can execute inside sandboxes and produce objective signals: exploit succeeded, privilege escalated, sensitive asset reached, alert correctly resolved, detection fired, remediation passed, or service remained available. The challenge is preventing reward hacking, unsafe real-world action and benchmark overfitting.

High-value environments include web exploitation, cloud misconfiguration, identity attack paths, malware triage, SOC investigation, detection engineering, phishing defense and secure code repair. Human experts remain essential for threat-model selection, ambiguous verdicts and novel attack chains.

## Diligence questions

- Does the agent execute actions or only recommend them?
- Are exploit findings proven with safe working evidence?
- What telemetry leaves the customer's environment?
- Which actions require human approval and how is rollback handled?
- Does learning mean weight updates, tenant memory, policies or retrieval?
- How are false-positive and false-negative rates measured on customer distributions?
- Can the system resist malicious instructions embedded in logs, tickets and code?
