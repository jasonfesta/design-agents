## Findings that change how we organize

- The two chats titled **design agent** have different purposes. Keep personal design craft and team/hiring research separate.
- Source snapshots intentionally duplicate older files. They retain historical evidence and hashes; they are not the active instructions to load by default.
- The skill catalogue mixes full local guidance with pointers. The main README and inventory now make that distinction explicit.
- A chat describing a proposed installable agent does not establish that a background service, auto-updater, or IDE integration was built.
- Product/platform/page-type taxonomy work was still being reconciled. Use the current taxonomy and taste coverage files instead of freezing another copy here.
- Missing category-specific taste must be disclosed. Research organization alone does not establish a preference or an approved Dream baseline.
- The old resources/research/team-watch automation was explicitly removed. Dream Mode's instructions were drafted without scheduling it. No schedules were changed during this organization pass.

This index is a navigation aid. Taste-specific recovery still requires extracting original wording and scoped feedback into the existing taste records; this pass does not claim that the entire research backlog has been classified or learned.
