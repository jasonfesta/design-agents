# apps style vocabulary

Working vocabulary v0.4 · October 12, 2026 · Type-specific exploration; fit unassessed

Use the 31 preferred terms and naming boundaries in [the shared vocabulary](README.md). The interpretations below are research proposals for apps. They do not imply every term fits every viewport or category, and they establish no personal preference. Historical influences, contemporary UI styles, and cultural aesthetics keep their distinct kinds even when combined in a direction.

## Type-specific interpretations

### Composition

| Preferred term | Stable ID | Proposed app interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimalism | `minimal` | Restrained navigation and clear task hierarchy. |
| Maximalism | `maximal` | Expressive discovery and media surfaces with bounded control areas. |
| Editorial | `editorial` | Feeds, readers, and content libraries with considered text/image hierarchy. |

## Motion and implementation

Use [motion terms](MOTION.md) to state exactly what moves, how it moves, and what triggers it. A visual style does not imply an animation technique. Distinguish an interactive interface from a rendered promotional video of that interface. Tool choice, platform design system/version, motion recipe, rendering, layout, palette, mode, and density stay inside the record.

For apps, document interaction states and repeated-use behavior. For websites, document content hierarchy, responsive behavior, and the relationship between scroll and motion. These requirements apply where relevant to the current type; the aesthetic name does not supply them automatically.
