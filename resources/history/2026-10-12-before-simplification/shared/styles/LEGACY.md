# Earlier shared style families

Historical vocabulary only. New type-specific lists are indexed in [README.md](README.md). Existing IDs remain resolvable; a treatment stays a field within the style record.


Earlier illustrative IDs included `websites/desktop/apps-shopping/editorial/archive-catalogue` and `apps/mobile/apps-finance/editorial/archive-catalogue`. Normalize them to `websites/desktop/shopping/editorial` and `apps/mobile/finance/editorial`, keeping `archive-catalogue` as treatment metadata. These examples have no populated research or approved baseline.

| Family ID | Style family | Treatment IDs and observable starting cues |
| --- | --- | --- |
| editorial | Editorial | `magazine`: deliberate headline/body contrast and paced sections; `archive-catalogue`: indexed objects, captions, metadata and stable framing; `photo-essay`: images determine reading rhythm. |
| minimal | Minimal | `quiet-gallery`: generous framing and restrained hierarchy; `geometric`: repeated proportions, few shapes, precise alignment. |
| utilitarian | Utilitarian | `index`: efficient scanning, aligned labels and explicit metadata; `instrument`: legible values, status and actionable controls; `workspace`: persistent navigation and repeatable work regions. |
| expressive | Expressive | `poster`: dominant focal type and strong graphic relationships; `collage`: composed layers and deliberate image juxtapositions; `type-led`: typography supplies the main visual character. |
| tactile | Tactile | `paper`: print-like surfaces, edges and captions; `physical-controls`: shape and state cues make operation tangible. |
| atmospheric | Atmospheric | `light-depth`: light and shadow establish layered space; `translucent`: bounded transparency and explicit backdrop rules; `cinematic`: controlled scene, contrast and image-led pacing. |
| organic | Organic | `botanical`: plant-derived forms and image relationships; `handmade`: intentional irregularity, drawn marks and crafted texture. |
| historical | Historical reinterpretation | `swiss-grid`: typographic hierarchy and rigorous alignment; `modernist-geometry`: geometry and compositional balance; `early-digital`: period-specific interface or display cues. |

These are search and exploration labels, not sufficient specifications. Every activated treatment needs 3–5 concrete identity rules, exclusions, reference evidence, and exact system decisions. Historical labels are research leads: verify period and source before claiming historical accuracy. A style may combine influences, but name one primary family and explain every secondary contribution. Avoid an unexplained hybrid of all supplied references.

Keep these as separate controls or tags: density, contrast, color temperature, corner geometry, imagery treatment, motion intensity, audience, and output format. Record viewport type in the taste path; page type remains a detail. “Luxury,” “friendly,” and “futuristic” are intent tags until translated into observable rules. A dashboard is a page type, not a style family. Glass can be a material in an editorial direction without redefining the entire family.
