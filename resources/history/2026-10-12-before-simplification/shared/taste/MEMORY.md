## Visual taste still being established

Jason's latest instruction requires defining taste for each branch from research before personal recommendations, and telling the user upfront when that research is missing. Follow [TASTE.md](../workflow/TASTE.md) and [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md). The design types are **apps, websites, and games**. Each contains **viewport → category → style**. Website design uses and app interface types replace the primary Google Play app/web menu. Jason approved both replacement category lists; styles remain unresolved and game genres are unchanged. Industry and finer page/screen details remain metadata. Jigs is retired. Approval of the structure does not approve a style.

The existing [catalogue](../../resources/research/library/README.md) records Jason's endorsement of Reelfolio, Inspora, 60fps, Mobbin, Land-book, Refero, Brand Guidelines, and Backgrounds Supply. These are useful research sources. They do not by themselves identify a universal typeface, palette, density, material, or motion treatment.

The [main brief](../agents/design.md) interprets the supplied collection as interest in tactile controls, glass, shaders, expressive motion, and implementation-ready references. Retain that as exploration context. Do not apply those qualities to every design as confirmed preferences.
