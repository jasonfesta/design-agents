A style's fit, personal preference, direction selection, and baseline approval are separate facts. Child progress does not populate siblings. A definition for a dashboard does not cover every other page type; record its actual scope and exclusions. Tutorial values, generated candidates, and earlier category/style examples do not count as taste research.
