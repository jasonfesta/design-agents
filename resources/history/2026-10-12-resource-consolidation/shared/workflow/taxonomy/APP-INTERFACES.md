# App interface categories

Category menu v1.0 · Approved by Jason October 12, 2026 · Styles remain open

Jason selected **interface types: dashboards, feeds, editors, players, booking flows** as the category approach. Classify the designed screen, workspace, or flow by its primary task. Finance, health, messaging products, and other industry/product labels become optional context rather than the main folder menu.

The four-layer path stays `apps/<viewport>/<category>/<style>/`. Browser-based software belongs here as well as native apps. Create approved category folders only for actual work. Earlier store-category scaffolding is preserved in Resources History.

## Record granularity and overlap

These 20 approved families are our consolidation of screens and flows; they are not Mobbin's exact taxonomy. A product can contain many of them. Store a reference once, assign a primary category for the task, and link secondary interfaces or steps by source ID. Keep the sequence together when the reference is a flow.

Use `calendars` for organizing time and `booking-flows` for making a reservation. Use `editors` for modifying content and `canvas-builders` when spatial construction is central. Use `dashboards` for an overview and `data-management` for working directly with records. Fine-grained components (buttons, cards, tabs) and states (empty, error, loading) remain metadata or component content.
