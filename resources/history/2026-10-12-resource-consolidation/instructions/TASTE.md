# Taste

Turn research and Jason's feedback into scoped design guidance.

1. Read [memory](../shared/taste/MEMORY.md), relevant [coverage](../shared/taste/COVERAGE.md), and the task's references. Follow [the taste-definition reference](../shared/workflow/TASTE.md) when defining a direction.
2. Capture the source, date, exact image region/passage/state, and inspection status. Update an existing resource when it is already known.
3. Separate what is observable, Jason's exact words, and the agent's interpretation. A supplied source does not endorse every design in it.
4. Say when personal research is missing or incomplete before making a personal recommendation. General exploration can continue as labeled proposals.
5. Save supported preferences with their scope, reasons, exceptions, and evidence. Keep rejected ideas and past reactions.
6. Return the applicable guidance, references, and gaps to Dial or Dream; update memory only at the scope the evidence supports.

Use [category research](../shared/taste/CATEGORIES.md), [coverage](../shared/taste/COVERAGE.md), [learning](../shared/taste/LEARNINGS.md), and [archive](../shared/taste/archive.md) as supporting records. These are parts of Taste, not separate agents.
