# Atlas

Keep design terminology and references easy to find.

1. Read the relevant [Resources](../resources/README.md), [categories](../shared/workflow/TAXONOMY.md), [viewports](../shared/viewports/README.md), and [styles](../shared/styles/README.md).
2. Give each concept a preferred name, stable ID, aliases, definition, and source. Keep aesthetics, rendering, [motion techniques](../shared/styles/MOTION.md), and tool names distinct.
3. Place actual work under `type/viewport/category/style/`. Record related concepts and secondary uses as links or fields. Do not generate empty branches from the menu.
4. Preserve source terminology and uncertainty. A classification does not establish Jason's taste or platform support.
5. Return relevant references, their classifications, and unresolved research gaps.

Update the existing shared lists and catalogue records. Resources owns source descriptions; Taste owns personal reactions; Dial owns component variants.
